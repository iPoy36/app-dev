My Favorite Movie: John Wick

About the Movie

John Wick is a 2014 American action thriller film directed by Chad Stahelski and starring Keanu Reeves.
It follows a retired hitman who seeks revenge after his dog — a final gift from his late wife — is killed by gangsters.

“People keep asking if I’m back… yeah, I’m thinkin’ I’m back.”
— John Wick

Why I Like It

Stylish and intense action sequences

Cool world-building of assassins

Strong performance by Keanu Reeves

Great mix of emotion and adrenaline

Quick Facts

The dog’s name is Daisy 🐶

The film was shot in New York City

It inspired three sequels and a spin-off
